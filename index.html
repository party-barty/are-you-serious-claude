Our Story

Craftsmen Dedicated to Automotive Heritage

Heritage Motor Works was founded by master restorer Frank Castellano in 1987 with a simple belief: every classic car deserves to be restored to its original brilliance -- or beyond. What started as a two-bay garage has grown into a 15,000 sq. ft. restoration facility staffed by some of the most talented fabricators, painters, and mechanics in the country.

We specialize in American muscle cars, European sports cars, and pre-war classics. Whether you need a factory-correct concours restoration or a custom restomod build, our team delivers results that win awards and turn heads.

What We Do

Complete Classic Car
Restoration Services

From bare-metal bodywork to final detailing, we handle every stage of the restoration process under one roof with uncompromising attention to detail.

Full Frame-Off Restoration

Complete disassembly, rotisserie frame restoration, and meticulous reassembly. Every nut, bolt, and component inspected, restored, or replaced to factory specification.

Paint & Body

Downdraft spray booth with precision color-matching technology. From correct factory colors to custom finishes, we deliver flawless paint that wins at the highest levels of competition.

Engine & Drivetrain

Complete engine rebuilds, transmission overhauls, and drivetrain restoration. We work with everything from flathead V8s to big block 427s, matching numbers or blueprinted performance builds.

Interior & Upholstery

Expert reupholstery using period-correct materials and methods. Leather, vinyl, carpet, headliners, door panels, and dash restoration -- all crafted to original or custom specifications.

Electrical & Wiring

Complete wiring harness replacement, gauge restoration, and electrical system upgrades. We can maintain factory-correct wiring or integrate modern reliability with a period look.

Custom Fabrication

In-house metal fabrication for rust repair, panel replacement, and custom modifications. From patch panels to complete floor pans, our fabricators work steel and aluminum with surgical precision.

How It Works

Our Restoration Process

A proven, transparent workflow that keeps you informed and involved at every stage of your vehicle's transformation.

1

Consultation

We assess your vehicle, discuss your vision and budget, and develop a detailed restoration plan with transparent pricing -- no surprises.

2

Disassembly & Assessment

Complete teardown with photo documentation of every component. We catalog all parts and provide a comprehensive condition report.

3

Restoration & Build

Our craftsmen execute the restoration with regular photo updates. You are welcome to visit the shop any time to see your car's progress.

4

Delivery & Unveiling

Final quality inspection, test drive, and a reveal event at our shop. We deliver your finished vehicle with a comprehensive documentation binder.
